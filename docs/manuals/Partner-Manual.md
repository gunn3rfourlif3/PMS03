# Locare — Partner Manual

A screen-by-screen reference for partners: what each page in the partner portal
is for, and a direct answer to the question people actually ask on it.

This is the **reference**. `LOCARE_PARTNER_CURRICULUM.md` is the **course** — it
teaches you to sell Locare, and it is what you read once, in order. This manual
is what you open mid-task when you want one answer and nothing else. Where the
two overlap, the curriculum has the reasoning and this has the answer.

> **The programme is not open yet.** Partner approvals are on hold until Locare's
> VAT number is issued. Everything below describes the portal as built; what it
> cannot tell you is when you can be approved.

## Getting approved

Apply at `app.locare.co.za/partner-apply`, as an individual or a business.

**What do I have to supply?**

| | Individual | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | ID or passport | Company registration (CIPC) + each director's ID |
| Address | Proof of address | Proof of business address |
| Banking | Bank confirmation letter | Bank confirmation letter |
| VAT | — | VAT certificate if registered |

**Why so much for a referral arrangement?** You receive payouts, so your
identity and banking are verified first — FICA and POPIA both apply. Nothing is
provisioned until you pass.

**How long does it take?** Review is manual. The outcome is approve, reject, or a
request for more information.

**What happens to my ID and banking details?** Encrypted at rest, and masked even
in Locare's own admin views.

**What does my status mean?** `pending` — applied, not yet reviewed, no login.
`active` — approved and trading. `suspended` — login retained, no new accruals;
Locare will have told you why.

## Signing in

Sign-in is **passwordless**. Enter your email, press send, and a six-digit code
arrives. There is no password to forget and none to leak.

**The code has not arrived.** Codes expire after five minutes — request a fresh
one rather than waiting. If none arrive at all, mail Locare: it is almost always
an email-delivery problem on the receiving side, and it is ours to fix.

## Overview

The landing screen. Six figures: **pipeline value**, **active deals**, **agencies
signed**, **demos this week**, **commission month-to-date** (pending and paid),
plus recent activity.

**What is pipeline value?** The sum of expected monthly subscription across your
*open* deals — the first five stages. It is your own estimate, not a Locare
forecast, and it changes when you edit a deal's expected MRR.

**Why is commission month-to-date lower than I expected?** It counts what has
accrued so far this month, and commission accrues only on money the agency has
actually paid. See Commissions.

## Pipeline

Where prospects live before they become agencies. Seven stages:
**Lead → Contacted → Demo → Trial → Proposal → Won → Lost.** The first five are
open.

**What do I need to add a deal?** Agency or prospect name, contact name, contact
email, expected units and expected MRR.

**Why won't it let me add another?** You may hold at most **20 open,
unconverted prospects**. Past that the portal refuses new ones until you close or
lose some, and older leads expire. Agencies that arrive through your referral
link are exempt — those are conversions, not reservations. Twenty is far more
than anyone genuinely working a pipeline holds at once; hitting the cap means
you are registering leads you are not working.

**When should I log a prospect?** The day you speak to them, not the day they
show interest. Attribution is first recorded wins, within 90 days of first
contact, and it is set once and never changes.

**What happens when I mark one Won?** The deal links to the created agency, and
commission takes over from there on that agency's actual payments.

**Do I have to give a reason when I mark one Lost?** Yes, and answer honestly. It
is the only data anyone has about why deals fail, and it feeds back into the
curriculum you were trained on.

## Activity

A log of your calls, emails, demos, notes and stage changes.

**Is this busywork?** No — it is the evidence behind a promotion to Partner, and
it is what the leaderboard is computed from. A demo nobody recorded did not
happen as far as your tier is concerned.

**What counts as an activity?** Call, email, demo, note, stage change, signup.
Stage changes and signups are recorded for you; the rest you log.

## Agencies

The agencies attributed to you, with their tier, unit count, status and join
date — and your referral link.

**How does an agency become mine?** Two ways, and only two. Your **referral
link** — automatic and unambiguous, so prefer it and send the link. Or a **named
registered prospect** logged in your pipeline with an agency name and a named
contact, which Locare confirms is a genuine introduction before it counts.
Saying you spoke to someone first is not attribution.

**Two of us spoke to the same agency. Who earns?** Whoever recorded it first,
inside the 90-day window. That is the whole rule, and it is why you log on the
day of the call.

**What can I see inside one of my agencies?** Their name, tier, unit count,
subscription and status. Nothing else. No tenants, no leases, no financials, no
landlord details — not restricted by policy, not available to your login at all.

**A prospect asked whether I can see their tenants.** Say it plainly: *"My access
shows me that you're a customer and what plan you're on. I cannot see your
tenants or your money."* It is true, and it is a stronger answer than any
assurance you could offer instead.

## Commissions

Every accrual, by period, with the agency, the basis MRR, your rate, the amount
and its status. Totals for pending, paid this month, and paid to date.

**What is commission calculated on?** The referred agency's **recurring
subscription revenue, excluding VAT**. Once-off and pass-through charges are
excluded.

**When does it accrue?** Only on payments **actually received** from the agency,
in the month they are received. Nothing accrues on an invoice that has been
raised but not paid.

**Why not pay on billing instead?** Because recovering money already paid to a
partner is the fastest way to poison a channel. Paying in arrears on collected
revenue means clawbacks never arise.

**When am I paid?** Statement on the **7th**, payout by the **15th**, both for
the prior month.

**Nothing was paid out this month.** Below **R250** the balance rolls over, and
is swept quarterly regardless — so it is delayed, never lost.

**What do the statuses mean?** `pending` — accrued, not yet approved.
`approved` — confirmed, due in the next run. `paid` — sent. `cancelled` —
reversed before payment, which happens when the underlying payment was reversed
or the accrual was found not to be commissionable.

**What is my rate?** It follows your tier, and your current rate is shown on
every line on this screen. The rate ladder, the qualification gates and the
reasoning are in `LOCARE_COMMISSION_STRUCTURE.md` — that document is the source
of truth, and this manual deliberately does not restate the numbers so the two
can never disagree.

**How long does it run?** Introducer accruals run 24 months per agency. Partner
and Reseller are lifetime — for as long as that agency keeps paying.

**Can I earn on my own agency?** No. No commission is payable on an agency you
control: yours, any entity where you or an immediate family member is a director,
member or beneficial owner, or any agency under common control with one of those.
It is checked at approval and again at accrual. A match between your contact
email and an owner of a referred agency is conclusive and the commission is
withheld. If you run an agency and want to use Locare, you are welcome to — you
pay for it like any other customer.

## Banking

Where your payout account lives. Enter it once, and confirm it after any change
at your bank.

**Why has my payout not arrived?** Missing or stale banking details are the most
common reason. A payout run cannot include an account it does not have.

**Who can see these details?** They are encrypted at rest and masked in admin
views, the same as your KYC documents.

---

## Leaderboard

Other partners' display names, headline metric, rank and movement.

**What can other partners see about me?** Your display name, your headline
metric, your rank and your streak. Never your pipeline, your contacts, your
agencies or your banking.

## Questions that come up on calls

**What does Locare cost?** Send them to the live pricing page and quote from it.
Prices are displayed VAT-inclusive; the commission basis is ex-VAT. Pricing has
been repriced once already and the live site is the only source of truth — a
partner quoting from memory is how the wrong Starter figure survived in a
partner email for days after a change.

**They want something not on the pricing page.** The Custom tier is quotable but
not published. Bring it to Locare rather than inventing a number.

**Do I charge VAT on my commission?** Rates are VAT-exclusive. If you are VAT
registered, Locare self-bills you. If you are not, you are paid the rate as
stated.

**Can they keep their own brand?** That is the product. The agency runs under its
own brand and domain; their tenants and owners never see Locare. It is worth
demoing rather than describing.

**Is their data separate from other agencies'?** Yes, and enforced by the
database itself rather than by application code. You can say that plainly.

**Something I cannot answer, or a complaint about money.** Escalate rather than
improvise. An answer invented on a call becomes a promise Locare has to keep.
