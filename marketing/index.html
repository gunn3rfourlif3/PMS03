FAQ

Questions, answered

The essentials below — for anything else, tap the chat or book a demo.

What is Locare?

Locare is a white-label platform that runs your whole property operation — leasing, rent collection, trust accounting, owner payouts, and tenant & landlord apps — all under your own brand and domain.

Who is it for?

Property agencies and landlords managing rentals in South Africa — from a handful of units to multi-branch portfolios.

How much does it cost?

Flat monthly pricing by portfolio size: Starter (1–12 units) R925/month, Growth (13–364 units) R2,660/month, and Scale (365+ units) R6,014/month. All prices exclude VAT at 15% — so Growth invoices at R3,059 including VAT. Growth adds white-label, custom domain, online payments, owner portal, trust accounting, the branded rentals site, docs/e-sign and reports/API. Larger or multi-country needs — talk to us for a Custom plan.

Can I use my own brand and domain?

Yes — Locare is fully white-label. Your logo, colours and your own domain, so tenants and owners only ever see your brand.

What’s included?

Leasing & tenant onboarding, online rent collection with reconciliation, deposit trust accounting & owner payouts, an owner portal, branded tenant & landlord apps, your own rentals site, documents & e-signature, and reports with CSV/API access.

How do tenants pay rent?

Tenants pay online and payments reconcile automatically to a proper double-entry ledger, using local South African payment methods.

Do you have mobile apps?

Shared Locare tenant and landlord apps are included. A fully branded native app for your agency is available as an Enterprise add-on.

How long does it take to get started?

You can be live in an afternoon — sign up, add your branding and domain, and start onboarding tenants. We help you through setup.

Can I migrate my existing data?

Yes — we help import your properties, units and owners during onboarding, so you’re not starting from scratch.

Is my data secure?

Each agency’s data is isolated, the platform is POPIA-aligned, and trust accounting is built to be audit-ready and PPRA-aware.
