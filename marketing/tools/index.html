Property calculators for South African agencies

Quick, free calculators for the sums rental agencies and landlords do every week. No sign-up, nothing stored — everything runs in your browser.

  • Rental escalation calculator Project rent year by year across a lease at a fixed annual escalation.
  • Deposit interest calculator Work out the interest owed to a tenant on a deposit held in an interest-bearing account.
  • Agency commission calculator Monthly and annual management commission on collected rent, with VAT.
  • Rent affordability calculator Check what rent an applicant can realistically carry against income and debt.
These calculators are provided for general guidance. They are not legal, financial or tax advice — confirm figures against the lease, the Rental Housing Act and your own accounting before relying on them.
