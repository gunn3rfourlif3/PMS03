Locare — two-minute product walkthrough

A white-label, multi-tenant property management platform for South African rental agencies. Each agency runs the full rental lifecycle under its own brand and domain — tenants and owners never see Locare.

1:52 1920×1080 Back-office · tenant app · landlord app Recorded from the running product

What you're looking at

Back-office

Next.js 14 App Router. Rent roll, proof-of-payment review, owner statements, and reporting with a real income statement.

Tenant & landlord apps

React Native (Expo), sharing one themed component library with the back-office. Both are white-labelled per agency.

Double-entry ledger

Every invoice, payment, deposit and payout posts through an immutable ledger. Corrections are new postings, never edits.

Tenant isolation

Enforced by PostgreSQL row-level security keyed on the vendor, not by application code.
